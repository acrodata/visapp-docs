### Canvas

The canvas supports the following component operations:

* Drag to move
* Scale adjustment
* Rotation
* Box-select multiple components

### Ruler

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Xnip2026-10-21_17-16-18.jpg" alt=""><figcaption><p>guidelines</p></figcaption></figure>

Ruler and guideline operations:

* Hold and drag the ruler to create a guideline
* Double-click a guideline to delete it
* Click the small eye icon in the top-left corner to hide all guidelines

### Toolbar

The toolbar provides canvas zoom buttons. Keyboard shortcuts are recommended for improved efficiency.
